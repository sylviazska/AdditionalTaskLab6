Findings:

How do group animals find food and their way, compared with loners?

- In our dataset, some group animals like gray wolves, bottlenose dolphins work together to catch prey
while loners like snow leopards hunt on alone.

- Group animals use different cues to find their way. For instance gray wolves use their sense of smell while
bottlenose dolphins use echolocation.

- Loners also use different cues like bold jumping spiders use vision while loggerhead sea turtles use Earth's magnetic field.
